DIGITAL PAYMENTS (DATA PROTECTION AND CYBER RESILIENCE) DIRECTIONS, 2026
Chapter III: Obligations of Regulated Entities

Para 7. Storage of Payment Data. The entire data relating to payment transactions shall be stored only in systems located in India. Copies, including backups and disaster-recovery replicas, shall not be maintained outside India.

Para 8. Retention of Records. Regulated entities shall preserve transaction records for a minimum period of five (5) years from the date of the transaction. ICT system logs shall be maintained securely for a rolling period of one hundred and eighty (180) days.

Para 9. Erasure. Personal data of a customer shall be erased within thirty (30) days of closure of the account, unless its retention is required by Para 8.

Para 10. Encryption. Customer data at rest shall be encrypted using AES-256 or an equivalent standard. Data in transit shall be protected using TLS version 1.2 or higher.

Para 11. Incident Reporting. Every cyber security incident shall be reported to the Board-designated authority and to the Regulator within six (6) hours of being noticed. Affected customers shall be informed without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours.

Para 12. Access Management. Privileged access to production systems shall be protected by multi-factor authentication and shall be reviewed at least once every quarter.

Para 13. Grievance Redressal. Every regulated entity shall publish on its website the name and business contact details of its Grievance Officer. Grievances shall be resolved within thirty (30) days of receipt.

Para 14. Consent. Consent shall be recorded with a timestamp for each specified purpose, and the customer shall be able to withdraw consent with the same ease with which it was given.
